Append-only. Each entry gives the date, the decider, the decision, the rationale and the consequences.

## DEC-001 (2026-11-24): Licence chosen by the owner

- **Decider:** the owner.
- **Decision:** All original material is licensed under the ECHO Source-Available Non-Commercial Research License v1.0 (SPDX `LicenseRef-ECHO-NC-Research-1.0`), with CLA v1.0.
- **Context:** Master prompt section 2 asks for an "owner-approved permissive code license". On 2026-11-24 the owner chose this protective, non-commercial licence instead, for their own protection.
- **Consequences:**
  - The licence files (`LICENSE`, `NOTICE.md`, `LICENSE_HISTORY.md`, `CLA.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `.github/`) are the owner's and are not changed by sessions.
  - The project is source-available, not OSI open source, and documents never call it "open-source".
  - Data licences are recorded separately in `data/manifest.json`.

## DEC-002 (2026-11-24): Synthetic data as the primary source (proposed by the AI, pending owner confirmation)

- **Decider:** the AI proposes; the owner confirms.
- **Decision:** Confirmatory experiments use a seeded synthetic catalogue and synthetic listeners. Last.fm 1K/360K data is excluded: it is non-commercial, it carries user identifiers and demographic profiles, and the master prompt forbids personal data without owner approval. The Million Song Dataset is excluded because its licence could not be verified. FMA metadata (CC BY 4.0) is an optional later exploratory extension, subject to owner approval.
- **Rationale:** No rights risk, no personal data, full redistributability, a five-day scope.

## DEC-005 (2026-11-24): Primary baseline and metric (PROPOSED, awaiting owner freeze)

- **Decider:** the AI proposes; the owner decides at freeze.
- **Proposal:**
  - Baseline: uniform-random recommendation (RAND). It is the simplest policy and does not use the listener's profile, so it is the natural "no steering" reference for induced shift (in the spirit of carroll2022).
  - Primary metric: mean angular preference drift.
  - Primary contrast: SIM vs RAND under adaptive preferences.
- **Alternative the owner may prefer:** POP as the primary comparator. It is the simplest of the three policy families named in the master prompt.
